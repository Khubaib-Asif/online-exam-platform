# M8 — Grading, Results & Audit — Future Scope

**Module:** M8 — Grading, Results & Audit
**Status:** v1 boundary; no open v1 requirements
**Authoritative references:** `docs/HIGH_LEVEL_DESIGN.md` §§9, 13; `docs/LOW_LEVEL_DESIGN.md` §16

## Deferred capabilities

- institution/class/course result aggregation;
- anonymous double marking and moderation workflows;
- external gradebook/LMS export;
- advanced analytics and cohort benchmarking;
- automated final decisions from AI; and
- student result appeals and regrade workflows.

## Boundary rule

Future grading features must preserve immutable submitted-attempt inputs, teacher authority over subjective marks, explicit publication, authenticated student visibility, and append-only audit history.
