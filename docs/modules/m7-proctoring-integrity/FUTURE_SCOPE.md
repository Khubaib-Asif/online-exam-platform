# M7 — Proctoring & Integrity — Future Scope

**Module:** M7 — Proctoring & Integrity
**Status:** v1 boundary; no open v1 requirements
**Authoritative references:** `docs/HIGH_LEVEL_DESIGN.md` §§9, 13; `docs/LOW_LEVEL_DESIGN.md` §15

## Deferred capabilities

- advanced face-recognition and liveness-provider integrations;
- automated disciplinary decisions without authorised review;
- multi-camera and external sensor orchestration;
- long-term behavioural modelling across exams;
- third-party proctoring-provider federation; and
- evidence export packages beyond the v1 authorised review projection.

## Boundary rule

Future proctoring capabilities must preserve minimal collection, encrypted storage, retention enforcement, versioned algorithms, reviewer auditability, and M6 authority over timing and session state.
